Privacy and a plain disclaimer

Your chat is kept in this browser for the visit. Shaley uses the current conversation to answer you. It is not saved as an account. Tap Start fresh, or close the session, and the chat stored here is cleared.

If you sign up, your email is saved only for the garden newsletter. It is not sold, and it is not used for anything else. A new signup can receive a short confirmation note. Later notes go out only when a newsletter is sent. Every note has an unsubscribe link, and that link removes your address. You can also write to hello@shaleygardens.com and ask to be removed. Addresses are not shown on the website.

Garden answers and the planting pages are general guidance based on University of Florida IFAS Extension publications. They are not a professional consultation, a soil test, or instructions for mixing a pesticide. Follow the product label, and ask the Marion County Extension office when you need local help.
